\recipe{Emily’s Brown Sugar Cookies}
\label{recipe:brown_sugar_cookies}
\makes{30-32 cookies}
\worktime{\SI{40} \minute}
\totaltime{\SI{50} \minute}
\source{Emily}


\begin{ingredients}

  \item 364g all-purpose flour
  \item 3/4 teaspoon bicarbonate of soda
  \item 3/4 teaspoon salt
  \item 227g unsalted butter at room temperature
  \item 350 g dark brown sugar
  \item 1 teaspoon molasses
  \item 1 large egg plus 1 large yolk
  \item 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
  \item 100 g granulated sugar for rolling
\end{ingredients}


\begin{method}
  	Adjust an oven rack to the middle of the oven. Preheat the oven to
180C. Line three baking trays with baking paper.

In a medium bowl, mix the flour, baking soda, and salt.

In a bowl, beat the butter on medium speed until creamy, then add
the brown sugar and the molasses and mix until light and fluffy, 2 to
3 minutes. Add the egg, yolk, and vanilla, and mix until combined.
Add the flour mixture mix until just combined.

Place the granulated sugar in a medium bowl.

Form the cookies into 1 1/2 oz [45 g] balls (2 tablespoons). Roll
each ball in the sugar and place 8 cookies on a sheet pan.

Bake one pan at a time, rotating halfway through baking. Bake until
the sides are set and the bottoms are light golden brown, 10 to 11
minutes.
  
\end{method}
