\recipe{Katie’s Brownies}
\label{recipe:brownies2}
\makes{8 Pieces}
\worktime{\SI{20} \minute}
\totaltime{\SI{40} \minute}
\source{Katie}


\begin{ingredients}

  \item 3 large eggs
  \item 275g caster sugar
  \item 175g salted butter
  \item 200g 54-60\% dark chocolate chopped into small pieces
  \item 100g 70\% dark chocolate chopped into coffee bean sized pieces.
  \item 175g plain flour
\end{ingredients}


\begin{method}
  	Pre heat oven to 180C. Line a 20 by 34cm baking tray with baking
paper.

Break the eggs into a mixing bowl, add the sugar and give a few
whisks with a hand wisk or electric beater, then set aside. This helps
the sugar to dissolve.

Put the butter in a pan and leave over a medium heat until it has
completely melted and small bubbles are just beginning to rise to the
surface. Be careful not to let it boil. Turn off the heat, then add all
the 54-60\% dark chocolate and a third of the 70\% chocolate. Stir
until melted.

Whisk the eggs a little more until they start to get paler, then whisk
in the chocolate mix to make a smooth paste. Sift in the flour and
stir gently with a spatula until combined. Finally stir in the
remaining pieces of chocolate.

Pour the mis into the lined tray and level with a spatula or palette
knife. Bake for 18-20 min until the edges are starting to rise and
crack slightly and the centre is soft to the touch but not liquid.
Remove from oven and leave to cool in the tray.

Cut into 8 large squares and serve.
  
\end{method}
